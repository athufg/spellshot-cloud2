# SPELLSHOT — Season 0 "The Fall": Full Lore Content Pack

*Written 2026-10-29. This is the actual, final in-game text for every lore touchpoint across Season 0's one-month cycle — not a summary of what each piece contains. Voice follows `SPELLSHOT_Lore_Ley_Sundering.md`'s formal chronicle register for the Codex entries, with each contributor (miner, priest, scholar) given a distinct voice against that baseline. Mapped to the delivery surfaces discussed this session: cinematic reveal, in-client Codex tab, map collectibles, cosmetic flavor text, Hub ambient barks, menu/post-match ticker, patch-note framing, loading screens, season-close recap.*

**Season 0 premise (locked):** the Ley fragment falls from the sky and lands; the Elemental Quarry opens as people mine it for Sparks. Three unnamed factions take shape in the background — the Hub's priesthood, the Quarry's miners/operators, and a scholarly faction tied to the chronicles — without any one of them being named as individual characters yet. Season 1 picks up with the first Unfound Core located.

**Fixed, 2026-10-30 — the landing site is a floating landmass, not ordinary ground.** The Elemental Quarry is canonically "a pit quarry on a floating island" (`SPELLSHOT_Design_Manual.md`, Section 4) — unremarkable sky-geography in this world, the same kind Skyline's cloud-sea setting already uses elsewhere, not something the Fall caused. The original cinematic draft never showed this and read as an ordinary ground-level valley as a result; fixed in the shot list below with an explicit wide establishing shot. Entry 1's distant witnesses (a watchman, a ferryman, a shepherd "three valleys away") didn't need rewriting — none of them claim to be standing on the landing site itself, only to have heard or felt something from elsewhere, which holds regardless of what the site itself is standing on.

**Fixed, 2026-10-30 — why anyone already knows what a "Spark" is on Day 1.** Owner correctly caught that the original draft had miners and buyers acting on knowledge they had no way to have, days after something nobody had ever seen before fell from the sky. Real fix, cross-checked against `SPELLSHOT_Lore_Ley_Sundering.md` and `SPELLSHOT_Design_Manual.md` (confirmed "Sparks" is already the game's locked persistent currency — this is that currency's in-fiction origin, not a coincidental reuse of the word): **"Spark" is not a new word and Echo-trading is not a new practice.** A Spark has always meant a minor, loose Echo — small, unshaped, not worth building into a full Technique on its own, but still genuinely tradeable, the way any Duelist has always been able to gather and carry an Echo whether or not they hold a Core. That's old, mundane, background economy — it's also, quietly, why "Sparks" is already slang for money generally in this world, well before Season 0. What's actually new on Day 1 isn't the concept or the value, it's the sheer concentrated *volume* — centuries' worth of scattered, rare finds suddenly sitting in one hole in the ground. That's the gold rush: everyone already knows exactly what they're looking at, which is precisely why they move this fast.

**Delivery cadence, decided 2026-10-30 — this is the template for every season from here on, not just a Season 0 patch.** The 4 Codex entries stay personal-pace-gated (unlock on Rift Pass tier progress), which is right for a lore reader but means nothing changes for anyone just because real time is passing. Owner flagged this would feel static over an actual month, correctly — so a second, parallel track is added: **World Events**, below, fire on the same real calendar day for every player regardless of individual progress, each pairing the existing ticker headline with real substance (a short vignette in its own voice, plus a concrete, noticeable change to the Hub — a new bark, a new prop) rather than being a bare headline. Assume a 28-day season for clean weekly math: Day 1 (launch, the cinematic), Day 8, Day 15, Day 22 (the three new World Events below), Day ~28 (season close). A returning player should be able to tell what day-range it is just from what's changed in the Hub, without checking any personal progress bar.

## 1. The reveal cinematic (Day 1, battle pass opening reveal)

Renders grounded/realistic, breaking from the game's pop-art comic UI for this one moment (per the locked split: UI chrome stays comic, anything depicting the physical world goes grounded/real) — per the already-locked pass mechanic, the outer teaser tile plays a comic-style GIF, and switches to this grounded render the moment the pass is opened.

**Shot list** (fixed 2026-10-30 — the landing site is the Elemental Quarry, canonically a floating landmass per `SPELLSHOT_Design_Manual.md` Section 4, the same unremarkable sky-geography Skyline's cloud-sea setting already establishes elsewhere in this world; a wide shot now makes that explicit on-screen instead of leaving a player to assume ordinary ground):
1. Black screen. Slow push-in on a night sky.
2. A tear of light opens — silent for a beat too long before the sound catches up.
3. Something vast and crystalline tumbles through cloud, trailing a fire that reads as the wrong color — too clean, closer to light than heat.
4. Wide shot, pulling back: the target is a floating landmass, drifting in open sky — establishes where this is before the camera commits to it.
5. Cut to its surface: a crowd looks up, goes still, then runs.
6. Impact. Whiteout.
7. Hold on black.

**Narration (spoken, plain documentary register — NOT the formal chronicler voice used in the Codex entries below; a narrator talking directly to the player reads differently than an in-world written document does):**

> *"It happened sometime after midnight — nobody can say exactly when. No warning, no signs. Something the size of a mountain just... fell out of the sky. People three valleys away heard it before they saw it. People standing right underneath it didn't hear anything at all. By morning there was a hole in the ground that hadn't been there the night before — and something sitting inside it nobody could explain. That's when everything changed."*

Title card. Season begins.

*(Revision note, 2026-10-29: earlier draft of this line used the formal "It is recorded, in every account that survives..." chronicler register — reserve that voice for the written Codex entries only. The spoken narrator line should always read as plain, normal spoken English, explaining what happened in sequence, the way a person would actually tell it.)*

## 2. In-client Codex entries (unlock via Rift Pass tier progression across the season — NOT calendar-gated by week; "Rift Pass," corrected 2026-10-30, is the actual locked name, not a generic "pass")

Revision note, 2026-10-29: these unlock as the player progresses through the season's pass tiers (play, earn XP, level the pass), the same as any other pass reward. They are NOT locked to specific calendar weeks — a player who levels the pass fast should be able to read all 4 in one sitting if that's how fast they progressed; a slower player gets them later, whenever their own tier progress gets there. Suggested rough spacing across a season's tier track (example assumes ~80 tiers, adjust to the real max): Tier ~5, Tier ~25, Tier ~45, Tier ~65. The "Week N" labels below are now just narrative/content order, not unlock gates.

### Entry 1 — "What Fell" (early Rift Pass tier)

> They say it did not fall so much as arrive — that the sky did not break so much as open, for exactly as long as it needed to, and no longer. Those who saw it agree on little else. A watchman on the northern wall swears it screamed on the way down, a sound with no mouth behind it. A ferryman three valleys south says he heard nothing at all, only felt the water go still beneath his boat for the length of a breath, as though the river itself had stopped to listen.
>
> What is agreed is this: something came down, and where it landed, the ground did not scar the way ground should. No crater, no scorch, no broken stone flung wide. Only a wound, clean-edged, already closing at the lip, as if the land itself were ashamed to be seen holding it open.
>
> By the second morning, the first tents were up.

### Entry 2 — a miner's account (mid Rift Pass tier)

> Don't let the scholars tell you it's cursed. I've had my pick in that ground eleven days now and I'm richer for every one of them. The dust gets into everything — your boots, your bread, your lungs if you're not careful — but it catches the light like nothing I've ever pulled out of a hillside, and the buyers in town don't ask where it came from twice.
>
> Old Beren won't go near the pit. Says he feels it looking back at him. I told him it's a hole in the ground, not a face, and he just about hit me.
>
> Maybe it is looking. I didn't come out here to find out. I came out here for the Sparks.

### Entry 3 — the Hub priesthood's complaint (later Rift Pass tier)

> We have asked, twice now, through proper channel and through plain plea alike, for the digging to stop until it can be understood. We have been answered with a shrug and a cart of ore.
>
> Let it be said plainly, so no one may later claim ignorance: what fell did not fall as stone falls. It has always been recorded that the Ley holds two faces, and that the Hub was given the keeping of its stillness. A stillness is not a resource. It is not a vein to be followed to its end.
>
> We do not ask that the Quarry close. We have asked only to be heard before what is taken from that ground cannot be put back. We do not expect to be heard. We are recording this so that, later, it will at least be known that we tried.

### Entry 4 — a scholar's note (final Rift Pass tier, season close)

> A sample, assayed properly for the first time this week rather than simply weighed and sold: the material does not behave as ore of any known kind. It does not corrode. It does not settle. Left sealed overnight, three separate samples had rearranged themselves by morning — not dramatically, not even consistently between samples, but measurably, and in no case by any means a draft or a tremor could explain.
>
> I do not yet have a theory I am willing to put my name to. I will say only this, and will deny saying it if asked: whatever fell is not finished happening.

## 3. Found collectible — torn ledger page (Hub prop, NOT on the ranked Quarry map)

Revision note, 2026-10-29: originally scoped as a physical pickup on the Elemental Quarry map. Moved to the Hub instead — the Quarry is a ranked duel arena, not an exploration space, and rewarding a player for stopping to interact with a world object mid-duel creates a real competitive-fairness problem (either everyone ignores it and it was wasted effort, or someone doesn't and eats a time cost that has nothing to do with skill, or its spawn location becomes exploitable meta-knowledge). The Hub is the social, non-competitive space where the ambient NPC barks already live and nobody's on a clock — a torn page sitting near the Quarry entrance or in a corner of the Hub does the same "world feels lived-in" job at zero competitive cost. Deliberately fragmentary — a found physical object, not a polished chronicle entry:

> *[...] third cart this week turned back at the gate, foreman's orders, no reason given. Pay docked for the lost hours. Half the crew's saying it's the priests leaning on him. Other half's saying he saw something in the last load he didn't like the look of. Nobody's asking him outright. Nobody wants the answer bad enough to risk the question. [...]*

**New, 2026-10-30 — a second Hub prop, appearing Day 15 (tied to World Event 2 below), not present before then.** A scorched support-beam fragment, pulled from the east-face collapse, left leaning near the Quarry entrance archway. Visual world-dressing primarily — if the engine supports a one-line inspect tooltip on Hub props, use: *"Pulled from the east face. Nobody's claimed it back."* If not, the prop's presence alone is the signal; no text is required for it to do its job.

## 6. World Events (NEW, 2026-10-30 — calendar-dated, same day for every player, replaces the old bare ticker-only treatment)

Each event fires on a fixed real day of the season, independent of individual pass-tier progress. Each pairs the ticker headline (unchanged from the original draft) with a short vignette in its own voice — distinct from the Codex entries' literary chronicle register, closer to an overheard notice or an incident log — plus a concrete, noticeable Hub change (new bark and/or new prop, both listed above) that starts that same day. This is what actually makes real time passing feel like something, rather than the season being four static Codex unlocks sitting untouched for weeks.

### World Event 1 — Day 8: "Spark prices double"

**Ticker:** "SPARK PRICES DOUBLE IN A WEEK — HUB MERCHANTS STRUGGLE TO KEEP PACE"

**Vignette** (posted outside the Cache vendor's stall, in a hand that isn't the vendor's own):
> *"Sparks: now paying double what we posted last week. We're not apologizing for the price. We're not keeping up with it either."*

**Hub change:** new bark enters rotation (above).

### World Event 2 — Day 15: "Minor collapse at the east face"

**Ticker:** "MINOR COLLAPSE AT EAST FACE — NO INJURIES REPORTED — WORK RESUMES BY NOON"

**Vignette** (Quarry incident log, East Face, filed same day — terse, bureaucratic, deliberately colder than the Codex voice):
> *"Partial collapse, east tunnel, approx. 0600. No injuries. Work resumed by noon per Foreman's order. Note: debris did not fall the direction the slope should have sent it. Logged. Not investigated further."*

**Hub change:** new bark enters rotation, and the scorched beam-fragment prop appears near the Quarry entrance (Section 3, above). A player who happens to read Codex Entry 3 (the Stillwatch's complaint) around this point sees it land with more weight — not a strict unlock-gate tie, just a deliberate consistency: the world just handed the Stillwatch's "it is not a vein to be followed to its end" line a piece of actual supporting evidence.

### World Event 3 — Day 22: "Hub priesthood requests audience — declined"

**Ticker:** "HUB PRIESTHOOD REQUESTS AUDIENCE WITH QUARRY OPERATORS — REQUEST DECLINED"

**Vignette** (notice posted at the Quarry gate, in the foreman's own hand — the operators have no formal name yet this season):
> *"The Hub sent robes again today asking for a sit-down. We sent back word: the ground's ours to work, same as it's been since the first tent went up. No audience. No committee. Business as usual."*

**Hub change:** new bark enters rotation (above). This is the last World Event before season close — the refusal that the Season 0 close screen refers back to ("not one of them got what they wanted").
