After is the live game: the 17 October pass, 2× hitboxes and one projectile speed for every M1 (200 studs/s). Before is the 16 October kit on the same model. In both, the duellists move while they shoot, targets jump, and any shot can miss. Change is after minus before. Both runs use the corrected M1 timing (18 October): the first published runs stepped in 0.1 s and fired fast M1s late (Wind Blade 40% slow, Chain Bolt 20%, Icicle Shard 17%), which understated Zephyr most.
