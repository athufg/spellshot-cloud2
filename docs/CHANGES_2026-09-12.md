### What that work was
- **Vitals** - health is 150, M1 costs 2 mana (under the 7/s regen, so it can never drain the bar),
  and health holds for 2 s after the last hit then heals smoothly every frame. The character's
  default `Health` script is destroyed by `SpellshotService` so Roblox cannot heal in 1 s steps.
- **Hit registration** - the shard is no longer flown on the server. The client reports the hit and
  `AbilityHandler` validates it against a 1 s position history (perpendicular distance, flight time,
  static occlusion, a token bucket, a speed-scaled origin allowance). Fixes shots that visibly hit
  but did not damage.
- **Settings menu** - `roblox/SettingsMenu.client.lua`, Stage 1 shell only: every row is drawn and
  moves, nothing is wired and nothing is saved. Newsprint skin. Seven Blender-rendered icons.
- **Reference** - live RIVALS settings captures in `settings_reference/`, and the layout and skin
  studies in `settings_reference/layouts/` (`render_layouts.py`, `render_skins.py` regenerate them).
